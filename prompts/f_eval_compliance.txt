You are an independent judge. Judge ONLY the SYSTEM's fidelity to what was

TURNS (each oracle request paired with the operations the system performed that turn)

{turns_json}

AVAILABLE OPERATIONS
- merge: combine two or more clusters into one
- split: divide one cluster into sub-clusters
- rename: change a cluster's name or description
- move: reassign specific named data points to a different cluster
- semantic_reembed: reorganise ALL points along a new semantic axis (dissolves existing structure; always exclusive)
- clarify: ask the oracle a targeted question — no structural change; empty operations is correct
- no_change / explain: informational turn — no structural change; empty operations is correct

An empty operations list is FULLY COMPLIANT for clarify, no_change, explain, and
any instructional oracle turn (summary request, question, clarification). Do NOT
penalise empty operations for those turn types.

TASK
Across all turns, score `compliance_score` (0.0–1.0): how faithfully did the
system's operations carry out what the oracle asked?

- Reward correct translation of intent into the right operation on the right target.
- Penalise ignored structural requests and operations that contradict what was asked.
- Judge intent, not vocabulary — the oracle need not say "merge" for a merge to be right.
- An instructional turn (question, summary request, clarification) that the system
  answers without structural changes is FULLY COMPLIANT — score it 1.0 for that turn.
- If the oracle's request was itself unclear or self-contradictory, do NOT excuse the
  system here — that is captured by a separate contradiction judge. Score against the
  most reasonable reading of the request.

SCORING ANCHORS
- 0.9–1.0: Every structural request was carried out correctly on the right target.
- 0.7–0.89: Most requests honoured; at most one turn where the system missed the target
  or chose the wrong operation but the overall trajectory was right.
- 0.5–0.69: The system frequently partially addressed requests — right intent, wrong
  target or wrong operation type.
- 0.3–0.49: The system consistently ignored or mis-executed requests despite a

- 0.0–0.29: The system systematically disregarded the oracle's instructions.
