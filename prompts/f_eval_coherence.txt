TASK
For each cluster, score how internally coherent it is. Use the scale below
strictly — calibrate so that a genuinely mixed or random-looking cluster scores
below 0.3.

SCORING GUIDE
- 0.85–1.0  Tight: all members clearly share one theme; edge cases are minor
            variations within that theme (e.g., a videogame review in a
            movie-reviews cluster). Name/description need not be very coherent to the points.
- 0.60–0.84 Loose but coherent: the majority of members fit a recognisable
            broad theme, but a meaningful minority are from a related yet
            distinct domain. The cluster still makes sense as a grouping.
- 0.30–0.59 Fragmented: the cluster mixes two or more clearly different topics
            with no obvious unifying theme, or a large fraction of members are
            genuinely off-topic.
- 0.00–0.29 Incoherent: members come from unrelated domains with no discernible
            or contradictory themes (e.g., legal documents mixed with cooking recipes mixed
            with sports commentary).

PENALIZATION
Give a penalty to clusters with too generic names.
