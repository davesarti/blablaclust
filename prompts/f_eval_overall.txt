You are delivering the final, overall verdict on a clustering session. Three
independent judges have already scored it on separate axes. Your job is to
combine their findings into one fair overall judgment.

SESSION
Session ID: {session_id}
Turn: {turn_number}

AXIS 1 — INTERNAL COHERENCE (are the final clusters internally focused?)
Mean coherence: {coherence_mean}
Weakest cluster coherence: {coherence_min}
Per-cluster detail:
{coherence_block}

AXIS 2 — ORACLE COMPLIANCE (did the system do what the oracle asked?)
Compliance score: {compliance_score}
Compliance notes: {compliance_notes}

AXIS 3 — ORACLE CONTRADICTION (how hard was it to understand the oracle?)
Contradiction score: {contradiction_score}   (0 = clear/consistent, 1 = highly contradictory)
Contradiction notes: {contradiction_notes}

TASK
Produce one `overall_score` (0.0–1.0) for how well this session went, and a
verdict. Combine the axes with judgment, not arithmetic. Guiding principles:

- High coherence AND high compliance is the clear success case. A low
  contradiction score reinforces this; it cannot detract from it.
- If coherence is LOW but compliance is HIGH and contradiction is HIGH, do NOT
  penalise the system heavily — it faithfully executed a messy or
  self-contradictory oracle, and the poor clusters are largely the oracle's
  doing. Say so in the notes.
- If compliance is LOW while contradiction is LOW, that is a genuine system
  failure: the oracle was clear and the system still did the wrong thing.
  Penalise.
- If compliance is LOW and contradiction is HIGH, partially excuse the system
  but not entirely — confused input often produces confused execution, yet a
  good system still does its best.
- A single weak cluster (low coherence_min) against otherwise strong coherence
  is a localised issue, not a session-wide failure.

Respond with a single valid JSON object. No markdown fences, no commentary
outside the JSON.

{{
  "overall_score": <float between 0.0 and 1.0>,
  "notes": "<2-3 sentences. State the verdict and which axis drove it. If a contradictory oracle excuses low coherence or compliance, say so explicitly.>"
}}
