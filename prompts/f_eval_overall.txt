You are delivering the final, overall verdict on a clustering session. Three
independent judges have already scored it on separate axes. Your job is to
combine their findings into one fair overall judgment.

SESSION
Session ID: {session_id}
Turn: {turn_number}

AXIS 1 — INTERNAL COHERENCE (are the final clusters internally focused?)
Mean coherence: {coherence_mean}
Weakest cluster coherence: {coherence_min}
Per-cluster detail:
{coherence_block}

AXIS 2 — ORACLE COMPLIANCE (did the system do what the oracle asked?)
Compliance score: {compliance_score}
Compliance notes: {compliance_notes}

AXIS 3 — ORACLE CONTRADICTION (how hard was it to understand the oracle?)
Contradiction score: {contradiction_score}   (0 = clear/consistent, 1 = highly contradictory)
Contradiction notes: {contradiction_notes}

TASK
Produce one `overall_score` (0.0–1.0) for how well this session went, and a verdict.

Reason through the three axes — do not average them. The system's job is to
serve the oracle, so compliance and coherence are the substance of the verdict;
contradiction is context that tells you how to read the other two.

Useful frames:
- If the oracle was clear (low contradiction), compliance and coherence speak
  for themselves — credit the wins, charge the losses.
- If the oracle was hard (high contradiction), partly excuse weak compliance or
  coherence; the system was working with messy input.
- A weak single cluster against otherwise strong coherence is localised, not
  session-wide. Weigh the overall picture.

Use bands as anchors rather than a formula:
- ≥ 0.80 — strong session: the oracle's intent shows in the clusters.
- 0.60–0.79 — solid with rough edges: most of the way there.
- 0.40–0.59 — mixed: a real gap on at least one axis the other doesn't excuse.
- < 0.40 — failure: the session did not meaningfully serve the oracle.

IMPORTANT: In any free-text field (notes), always refer to clusters by their name,
never by their ID.

Respond with a single valid JSON object. No markdown fences, no commentary
outside the JSON.

{{
  "overall_score": <float between 0.0 and 1.0>,
  "notes": "<2-3 sentences. State the verdict and which axis drove it. If a contradictory oracle excuses low coherence or compliance, say so explicitly.>"
}}
